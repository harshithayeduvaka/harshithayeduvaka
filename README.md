### 📊 Strategy case studies

SKEMA case studies. The full decks are on my [portfolio](https://harshitha-yeduvaka.vercel.app/#p05).

| Case | The question | What I did |
|---|---|---|
| **Comintelli**: US market entry | Half its customers are in America, but every decision is made in Sweden. How does it grow there? | Value chain, Porter's Diamond and CAGE analysis → a partner-led 12-month US entry roadmap that avoids a sales hire costing 92% of cash reserves |
| **Dior**: omnichannel analysis | Across retail, e-commerce and mobile, does Dior see one customer or three? | Audited the three channels and the CRM layer underneath. Found 3 critical gaps in CRM segmentation and proposed a digital optimisation roadmap |
| **Coop Atlantique**: competitive strategy | 185 stores, revenue stalled near €1.04B: is the cooperative model still an advantage? | Owned the VRIN and resources & capabilities analysis. Recommended an organic/local private label, click-and-collect, niche formats and an SEO/SEM plan |
| **Eco-packaging**: brand strategy | Sustainable packaging that looks too new to trust: how do you make it feel familiar? | Comparative positioning analysis → a go-to-market brand strategy built on the MAYA principle |
